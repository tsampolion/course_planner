# Lead book (2.8.0): implementation plan

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-custom-plans-roadmap-design.md`, PR3. **Brief:** `docs/CUSTOM_PLANS_BRIEF.md`, decision 7. JSON version 6.

- [x] Model: `SchoolClass.lead`, `Plan.lead` (passed through by `planFor`) and `Book.units` (unit start pages, read with `parseRanges`), all with defaults.
- [x] Engine (`engine/Lead.kt`):
  - `followLead` runs after the proposed allocation in `GroupReport.rows`, and after the custom-plan branch.
  - The lead keeps its own allocation. A follower without a finish-by date ends lesson *i* at `(2·c_i·n_B + n_L) / (2·n_L)`, where c_i is the lead's cumulative pages after lesson *i*, n_L its pages left and n_B the follower's.
  - With the same number of units, the lead's place inside its unit maps to the same fraction of the follower's unit; from the lead's last lesson with pages onwards, the follower is finished. With different counts it falls back to proportional, and `leadProblems()` explains why.
  - No lead, an unknown one, or a lead with no pages left leaves the allocation untouched.
- [x] `repaceFrom` follows the lead too: `followLead` with the pools left to re-pace. Re-pacing an untouched plan from its first lesson still equals the proposed plan.
- [x] Data: `JsonCodec.VERSION` = 6 (`lead` and `units` are written only when set). `Sanitise.kt` clears a lead that names no book of the class. `removeBook` clears it too.
- [x] UI:
  - Class screen: a "Lead book" picker when the class has two or more books, with a hint; *Leads* and *n units* tags on book tiles; and a "Unit start pages" field in the book sheet.
  - Group Stats: a *Leads* tag in Books.
- [x] Tests (`LeadBookTest`):
  - no lead changes nothing;
  - followers match the lead when proportions agree, and track its rounding (23 against 7 pages);
  - units link the follower, and a unit mismatch falls back to proportional;
  - a follower with a due date keeps its own pace;
  - re-pacing a custom plan follows the lead.
